Normada

A modular room divider, 3D-printed in wood-plastic composite. It invites exploration and looks different in every setup, with built-in storage, display space and a play of light.

Client
Normada (Swedish brand)
My role
Project leader
Team
Group project, LTU, 2023
Material
3D-printed wood-plastic composite

Divide, display, glow

Together with the Swedish brand Normada, we designed a 3D-printed room divider. The modular system lets every installation take a unique shape, while the modules double as storage and display, with integrated lighting adding an extra layer.

This was a group project in which I took on the role of project leader: planning the work, keeping the team aligned and making sure ideas kept moving towards a buildable result.

Inspiration

We set out to discover the frustrations of every stakeholder, not just the end user. These frustrations became the user needs that guided the concept.

Users
Movement distractions, unattractive environment
Assembly personnel
Complicated assembly, heavy and cumbersome dividers
Purchasers
Poor-quality dividers, hard to match colour and shape

Concept development

From a wall of quick sketches, the hexagonal module emerged as the strongest direction: it stacks in endless configurations and lets light through. Small 3D-printed test pieces and a scale model of an office space helped us evaluate form, proportion and how the divider would feel in a real room.

The final divider

The final system combines solid ribbed modules for privacy with open, lit cells for storing and displaying objects. Each wall can be configured differently, so no two installations look the same.
